The process

From bare ground to green cover in three steps

01

Report an empty spot

Spotted a bare roadside, median or vacant plot? Drop a pin and a short note — our team verifies it within 48 hours.

02

Join a planting drive

Sign up for a nearby drive. We bring saplings, tools and guidance — you bring your hands and a couple of hours.

03

Track it as it grows

Every sapling gets a tag and a spot on our map. Follow up on watering schedules and watch it grow over seasons.

Why it matters

Pakistan needs its green cover back

Forest cover here has fallen below 5% of total land — well under the 12% global recommendation. Roadside and urban planting is one of the fastest ways to reverse that.

🌡️

Cools city streets

Tree-lined roads can lower surface temperatures by several degrees in summer.

💨

Clears the air

A single mature tree filters dust and pollutants from thousands of litres of air daily.

💧

Holds the soil

Roots prevent erosion and help groundwater recharge during monsoon rains.

🏘️

Builds community

Neighbours plant, water and care for trees together — turning streets into shared spaces.
