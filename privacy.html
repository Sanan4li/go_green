Privacy Policy

Green Pakistan ("we", "us", "our") runs a community reforestation platform. This policy explains what information we collect from volunteers and visitors, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Last updated: July 11, 2026

1. Information we collect

When you create an account, report a bare spot, or sign up for a planting drive, we collect:

  • Account details — your name, email address, phone number, and city.
  • Volunteer activity — drives you've joined, spots you've reported, saplings you've been assigned to.
  • Location details — the pin you drop for a bare spot, and the general area of drives you attend.
  • Device & usage data — browser type, pages visited, and approximate location from your IP address, collected automatically.

2. How we use it

We use the information above to:

  • Match you with planting drives and volunteer opportunities near you.
  • Verify reported spots and coordinate sapling distribution with local partners.
  • Send you drive dates, watering reminders, and account-related notifications.
  • Understand overall platform usage so we can improve the experience.
  • Keep the platform safe and prevent misuse of reporting tools.

We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

3. Location data

Pins you drop for bare spots are shown on our public map so other volunteers and city partners can find and plant them. Please avoid including personal details (like your home address) in a report unless the spot itself is on your own property. You can request removal of a pin you submitted at any time by contacting us.

4. Sharing your information

We share information only in these situations:

  • Local drive organizers — your name and contact details, so they can coordinate the drive you've joined.
  • Service providers — tools we use to send emails, host our platform, or process sign-ups, bound by confidentiality obligations.
  • Legal requirements — if required by law or to protect the safety of our volunteers.

5. Cookies

We use basic cookies to keep you signed in and remember your city preference. We don't use cookies for third-party advertising. You can disable cookies in your browser, though some features — like staying signed in — may stop working properly.

6. Data retention

We keep your account information for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, we remove your personal details within 30 days, except where we're required to keep records for legal or safety reasons — for example, a history of spots you've reported may be retained in de-identified form.

7. Your rights & choices

You can, at any time:

  • Access or update your account details from your profile settings.
  • Request a copy of the information we hold about you.
  • Ask us to delete your account and associated personal data.
  • Opt out of non-essential emails using the unsubscribe link in any message.

To exercise any of these, reach out via the contact details below.

8. Children's privacy

Green Pakistan is intended for volunteers aged 13 and older. School and youth group drives may involve younger participants, but in those cases we collect information through the supervising teacher or guardian rather than directly from the child.

9. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures — including encrypted connections and access controls — to protect your information. No method of transmission or storage is perfectly secure, so we can't guarantee absolute security, but we work to keep your data safe.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as Green Pakistan grows. If we make significant changes, we'll notify you by email or with a notice on this page. The "Last updated" date above always reflects the most recent version.
